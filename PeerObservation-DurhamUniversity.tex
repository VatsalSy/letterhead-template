\small
For academic staff, this observation proforma may also be completed in the Physics database.\\[1ex]

University guidance on peer observation of teaching can be found at:\\
\href{https://www.dur.ac.uk/learningandteaching.handbook/7/5/4/}{\texttt{https://www.dur.ac.uk/learningandteaching.handbook/7/5/4/}}\\[2ex]

If you are being observed, you may wish to agree in advance with your observer which aspects of the session you would particularly like feedback on.

If you are observing, try to sit where you can see both the students and your colleague. You may, for example, wish to consider: inclusivity; rapport and student focus; content and structure; audibility and clarity; pace and timing; use of visual aids; and the quality and relevance of examples.\\[3ex]
