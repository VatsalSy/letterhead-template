\small
The University guidelines on the Peer Observation of Teaching are available at:\\
\href{https://www.dur.ac.uk/learningandteaching.handbook/7/5/4/}{https://www.dur.ac.uk/learningandteaching.handbook/7/5/4/}

If you are being observed, you may wish to discuss with your observer, before the event, those aspects on which you would specifically like feedback.

If you are observing, you will find it helpful to sit so that you are in a position to be aware of student reactions, as well as observing your colleague. The following are examples of things for which you may wish to look: inclusivity, rapport and student focus, content and structure, audibility and clarity, pace and timing, presentation and use of visual aids, examples, etc.
